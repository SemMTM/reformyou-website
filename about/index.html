Why ReformYou exists

A coaching program built for long-term results.

ReformYou was born from a simple observation: most people could follow a short-term plan, but very few were given the structure or clarity to maintain their progress afterwards.

From the beginning, the aim has been to offer a different approach—one that respects the reality of busy lives while helping people move towards their long-term potential.

Meet your coach

Experience that changed the way Dom coaches.

Dom started coaching over a decade ago, working with everyone from complete beginners to high-performing professionals. Before coaching, he competed as an athlete until a serious injury changed everything.

Recovering became a turning point. He had to rebuild not just strength, but confidence, habits, and overall health. That journey shaped the way he coaches today: steady, structured progress and an understanding that real change takes time.

Dom has since worked with more than 600 clients, contributed to workplace wellbeing programmes, and previously built and sold a nutrition company. ReformYou brings that experience together into one long-term coaching philosophy.

Clarity, structure, and long-term thinking.

The program is grounded in practical principles that make physical transformation easier to sustain through changing schedules and seasons of life.

  1. 01

    Consistency over extremes

    Real change comes from repeatable actions, not short bursts that are impossible to maintain.

  2. 02

    Small habits that compound

    Sustainable routines create results that can last long after the initial coaching phase.

  3. 03

    Honest, personal support

    Coaching works best when it responds to the person, their circumstances, and what the data is showing.

  4. 04

    Health that supports life

    Training and nutrition should strengthen your life rather than take control of it.

  5. 05

    Measured, patient progress

    Progress should be visible and measurable, but never rushed at the expense of long-term wellbeing.

Built around reality

An understanding of busy lives.

Most ReformYou clients are professionals balancing demanding careers, families, and long hours. Coaching has to adapt to real life, not the other way around.

That understanding shapes every part of the program: sessions that make sense, guidance that feels achievable, and habits that remain realistic over time.

The work I do is grounded in the belief that people are capable of far more than they think, especially when given structure and support. ReformYou is my way of helping people build that version of themselves sustainably.

Dom Oddy, founder
